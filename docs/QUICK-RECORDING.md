# Quick recording

The Samsung Side button picker exposes **Ramble Record**. This launcher lives in its own task, separate from MainActivity and the settings screens. A double press launches that entry and toggles the microphone foreground service; the transient activity removes its own task, returning to the previous foreground screen. A second double press stops and queues the recording, including from a sleeping/locked device. The ordinary Ramble launcher never starts recording.

A microphone foreground service captures AAC/M4A audio while the app is backgrounded or the display is asleep. It never restarts capture after a process kill or at boot. Finished recordings use AppRecordings and the normal transcription/export queue without replacing the onboarding trial ID. Pending finalization/queue insertion is recoverable and idempotent; incomplete partials are retained. Policy duration/file-size limits apply.

With the explicitly granted Android **Appear on top** / SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW permission, RecordingOverlay shows a small waveform/timer indicator above other apps. It is non-focusable and draggable. Tapping opens Ramble's live recording page with elapsed time and Stop and save, without toggling capture. The small overlay hides while that page is visible so controls do not overlap. It is removed on Stop, service teardown, screen off or keyguard lock. Without overlay permission, recording still works with the persistent system notification. Setup requests microphone/notification permission and opens Android's overlay permission screen before Side button settings.

On the locked phone, a public recording notification contains a system chronometer and Stop action; no transcripts or personal content are exposed. Samsung's lock-screen preferences control whether it initially appears as an icon or a card. The overlay does not draw above or bypass the keyguard. Opening the full app obeys the device's lock; the dedicated key entry can stop capture above the lock screen without exposing the app's other data.

Validation on real Remote Test Lab Galaxy S24 SM-S921B-UK01, Android 14: Ramble Record is selectable in the native Side button picker; first and second pairs of injected POWER key events start and stop capture; capture continues with the display asleep. RecordingOverlayTest and SideButtonRecordingTest passed on the device. Manual UI verification confirmed the previous Home screen stays visible, the small indicator persists, tapping opens the live recording page, and the locked phone exposes the native timer notification. No recording was left running after verification.
